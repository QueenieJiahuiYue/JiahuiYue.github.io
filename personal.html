Keeping the moments I want to remember

📸 I think beautiful moments are worth keeping — especially the ordinary ones I share with friends and family. I also believe reality is shaped by memory. When I revisit, select and edit photos or videos, I'm not simply documenting what happened; I'm shaping how I remember it. That process feels creative, personal and very much my own.

Meditation, anywhere

Reset · no perfect setting required
I like meditation partly because it does not require a perfect setting. I can do it almost anywhere — even next to rabbits. For me, it is less about creating a special atmosphere and more about practicing the ability to return my attention to the present.
