About

I'm a Modern East Asia Studies master's student who works comfortably between Chinese and German contexts — academically, professionally, and in daily life. My work tends to sit on a bridge: comparing pension systems across two countries, coordinating a program for people arriving from elsewhere, or filming what it's like to build a life in a new language.

I like taking something dense and making it legible. Whichever form it takes — a comparative table, a slide deck, a handout, or a two-minute video — the goal is the same: help someone understand quickly.

Selected work

/01

Legitimacy across systems: China & Germany

Comparative research Political science

A comparative analysis of how the Chinese and German pension systems justify themselves to their citizens — where their legitimacy comes from, and how differently each system earns public trust. Produced as reference tables, a presentation, and a structured handout for an academic audience.

Frameworks: Beetham's three-dimensional model of legitimacy · Scharpf's input / output legitimacy distinction

/02

UACP program launch

Program & comms Coordination

Helped shape the launch of the UACP program — from the kickoff mailer to the tracking system that kept the team aligned. Wrote and refined the communications that set the tone for a new cohort and coordinated moving parts across a small team.

Role: Communications design · project tracking · cross-team coordination

/03

Building a life in a new language

Content & storytelling Short-form video

An ongoing short-form video series documenting my evolving relationship with Germany over time — honest, unfiltered, and built around real moments rather than a polished highlight reel. Shot and edited end to end: concept, filming, and cut.

Toolkit: DJI Pocket 3 · CapCut · publish-first, refine-later
